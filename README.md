# Hi there 👋 We are Cerberus.

## Who we are
We are Cerberus, a student-led non-profit engineering team dedicated to building competitive hardware, competing in FTC, and expanding STEM education access.

## What we build & work on
* **Competitive Robotics:** Custom hardware design, rapid prototyping, and CAD modeling focused on performance and reliability.
* **Open-Source Tools:** Digital platforms and mapping tools (such as, but not limited to [Cal Connect FTC](https://calconnectftc.org)).
* **Community Outreach:** Hands-on technical workshops, youth summer academies, and STEM exhibitions covering 3D printing, CAD, and software concepts.

## Tech & Tools
* **CAD & Hardware:** Onshape, FDM 3D printing, CNC machining, custom laser fabrication
